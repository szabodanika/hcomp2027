Virginia Tech has negotiated special rates and corporate discount codes with several nearby properties in Crystal City / National Landing and Historic Old Town Alexandria. When booking, please use the provided rate codes or booking links to access these discounted rates.

Getting Around from Your Hotel

All recommended hotels offer convenient access to Academic Building One (AB1). For full details on regional transit, airport routes (DCA, IAD, BWI), local DASH bus options, Capital Bikeshare docks, and on-site parking rates, please visit our dedicated Conference Venue & Transportation Page.

  • On Foot: Hotels in Crystal City/National Landing (such as the Renaissance and Hyatt Regency) are within an 8- to 11-minute walk to AB1.
  • Via Metrorail: Properties near the King Street–Old Town station (Blue/Yellow Lines) are just two stops away from the Potomac Yard–VT Metrorail Station (~5 minutes on the train), which sits less than a 5-minute walk from the venue.
  • Rideshare & Taxi: Rideshare services (Uber/Lyft) are plentiful across Alexandria and Arlington, with trips to AB1 typically taking 5 to 12 minutes.
