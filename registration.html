Registration is Now Open

Registration is now open for HCOMP + CI 2026.

Register via CVENT

Workshops, CrowdCamp, and Doctoral Consortium will take place throughout the day on Sunday, Sep 27. The full conference begins with a reception on Sunday evening, Sep 27 and continues through Wednesday, Sep 30.

Conference Registration Fees

For the Full Conference (Sep 27-30), we have registration categories for ACM members, non-member professionals, and students. Student registrations must provide proof of student status.

All fees in USD.

Registration Type Early (Extended to Aug 28) Regular (Ends Sep 4) Late (After Sep 4)
ACM Member (Full Conference) $500 $550 $600
Non-Member (Full Conference) $600 $650 $700
Student (Full Conference) $300 $350 $400

Notes: At least one author of accepted papers and talks must register for the full conference.

Full conference registration covers main conference events on Sep 27-30 and includes snacks and beverages and one reception ticket for the evening of Sep 27.

When registering, attendees can choose to purchase extra tickets for the conference reception for $90 each.

Workshop Registration Fees

For the Workshop day (Sep 27), which includes Workshops, CrowdCamp, and Doctoral Consortium, we also have Workshop registration categories for ACM members, non-member professionals, and students. Participants can register for individual workshops without registering for the main conference. See the list of accepted workshops. Student registrations must provide proof of student status.

All fees in USD.

Registration Type Early (Extended to Aug 28) Regular (Ends Sep 4) Late (After Sep 4)
ACM Member $125 $150 $175
Non-Member $175 $200 $225
Student $90 $100 $110

Notes: The fee is the same for half-day or full-day workshops, but if you are attending two half-day workshops, you only need to register for the Workshop day once. Workshop registration includes snacks and beverages and a boxed lunch.

Policies & Accommodations

Cancellation Policy

No refunds except in the case of visa denial. If your visa is denied, you will be refunded the full registration amount less a $50 processing fee. Notification of cancellations should be made by email to hcomp-ci-2026-register@acm.org.

Special Accommodations

If you need any special accommodations or if you have dietary restrictions, please describe your needs when registering through CVENT. If you have any questions about registration, hotel, transportation, or anything else, please contact the Local Arrangements Chairs at hcomp-ci-2026-local@acm.org.
