Policies & Accommodations

Cancellation Policy

No refunds except in the case of visa denial. If your visa is denied, you will be refunded the full registration amount less a processing fee (amount To Be Announced). Notification of cancellations should be made by email to the Registration Chairs (contact details To Be Announced).

Special Accommodations

If you need any special accommodations or if you have dietary restrictions, please describe your needs when registering through CVENT. If you have any questions about registration, hotel, transportation, or anything else, please contact the Local Arrangements Chairs (contact details To Be Announced).
