Call for Student Volunteers

 

 

Student Volunteers (SVs) are an essential part of HCOMP/CI 2027. They help ensure the conference runs smoothly by supporting registration, session management, technical support, speaker coordination, and other on-site activities. In return, SVs have the opportunity to engage with the conference community, network with researchers and practitioners, and gain behind-the-scenes conference experience.

We invite applications from undergraduate, master’s, and Ph.D. students from all backgrounds and disciplines. No prior Student Volunteer experience is required; we are looking for enthusiastic, reliable, and collaborative volunteers.

Eligibility

Applicants must:

  • Be enrolled as an undergraduate, master’s, or Ph.D. student during the 2027–2028 academic year.
  • Be able to attend the conference in person for its full duration.
  • Commit to completing assigned volunteer duties and attending any required orientation sessions.

What Will I Do as a Student Volunteer?

All Student Volunteers agree to:

  • Commit to approximately 20 hours of volunteer duties during the conference (dates To Be Announced).
  • Attend the mandatory Student Volunteer orientation.
  • Arrive on time for assigned shifts and complete all assigned responsibilities.
  • Be a welcoming and professional representative of HCOMP/CI 2027 throughout the conference.
  • Arrange and cover their own travel, accommodation, and meals not provided by the conference.

Student Volunteer Benefits

Selected Student Volunteers will receive:

  • Complimentary conference registration
  • An exclusive HCOMP/CI 2027 Student Volunteer T-shirt
  • Opportunities to network with researchers, practitioners, and fellow student volunteers
  • Behind-the-scenes conference organization experience
